Curbside & Delivery Services

MARKETING RESEARCH

H-E-B Curbside & Delivery

Research proposal on whether H-E-B's curbside and delivery service quality drives customer satisfaction and loyalty.

THE 30-SECOND VERSION
  • The question: Do order accuracy, on-time pickup/delivery, and app usability drive satisfaction, and does satisfaction keep customers loyal against Walmart, Target, and Amazon Fresh?
  • Why now: U.S. online grocery hit $9.7B in March 2025, up 21% year over year. Missing items and substitutions are the top complaint.
  • The design: 5 hypotheses with satisfaction as the link between service quality and loyalty, tested by a 13-question survey of 100–150 Texas adults who used the service in the last 6 months.
  • The payoff: Shows H-E-B where fulfillment breaks down, guiding spend on fulfillment tech, substitution communication, and app fixes.
